03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might ask.

Keeping it simple. Just like signing up.

What is unimarkaz?+

UniMarkaz is an upcoming student marketplace in Pakistan. It’s a place to buy and sell books and everyday products, offer your skills, and find shared rides with other students. We’re bringing students from different campuses together. More on that when we launch.

Can students from my university join?+

Yes! University and college students across Pakistan can join the list, from Lahore, Karachi and Islamabad to Rawalpindi, Peshawar, Faisalabad and beyond. We’ll let you know when unimarkaz is ready for your campus.

Is it free to join the list?+

Completely free. All you need is your email. No password, student ID or payment details required to get early access to Pakistan’s student marketplace.

When will unimarkaz launch?+

We’re launching soon, but haven’t set a date yet. Students on the list will get invites before we open to everyone. Sign up and we’ll email you when you can join.

How do I get into the top 50?+

We’re accepting only the top 50 on the list for our first early-access group. Sign up, then share your invite link. More friends who sign up through it means a higher place; if two people have the same number of invites, the earlier signup comes first. We review signups and count each email only once. The registered user count shows the total on the list, not your personal position. Everyone else stays on the list for future invites.

What happens to my email?+

We use your email to send you an invite and news about the launch. SplitForms, our signup service, receives your email and invite code. If a friend invited you, we also send their code to count the invite. We don’t save your email in this browser or track you for ads. Reply to any update to leave the list.
