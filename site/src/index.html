HAVE A QUESTION?

Common questions.

Here’s what you need to know.

What is unimarkaz?+

unimarkaz is a place for students in Pakistan to buy, sell and find what they need from other students. We’re getting ready to launch and will share more details soon.

Can students from my university join?+

Yes. University and college students anywhere in Pakistan can sign up. We’ll let you know which campuses can use unimarkaz as we launch.

When will unimarkaz launch?+

We’re launching soon, but haven’t set a date yet. Students on the list will get invites before we open to everyone. Sign up and we’ll email you when you can join.

How do I move up the list?+

Sign up, then share your invite link. Each new friend who signs up through it can help you get an earlier invite. We check signups before sending invites and count each person’s email only once. Sharing a link without new signups won’t move you up. Your position on the list isn’t shown here.

What happens to my email?+

We use your email to send you an invite and news about the launch. SplitForms, our signup service, receives your email and invite code. If a friend invited you, we also send their code to count the invite. We don’t save your email in this browser or track you for ads. Reply to any update to leave the list.
