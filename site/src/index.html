THE SMALL PRINT, BUT READABLE

Good questions.

We figured you’d have a few.

Okay, but what actually is unimarkaz?+

A marketplace made for student life in Pakistan. Think what you have, what you need, and the people around your campus. We’re keeping the full reveal for launch. A little mystery never hurt anyone.

Is my university invited?+

Yes. University and college students across Pakistan are welcome on the waitlist. Big city, smaller campus, hostel or home — you belong here. We’ll share campus availability as we roll out.

When’s the big reveal?+

Launching soon. We’re getting things ready and will invite the early crew before the public launch. Leave your email and you’ll hear from us when access is ready. No made-up countdowns. We’ve had enough deadline anxiety.

How do I move up the list?+

Join, then share your personal invite link. When a new friend signs up through it, we record the referral. We review successful, distinct signups when arranging early-access invites: more genuine referrals mean higher priority. Sharing alone doesn’t move your spot, and we don’t display a live rank.

What happens to my email?+

We use it for unimarkaz early-access and launch updates. SplitForms processes your signup, including your invite code and a friend’s code if you arrived through their link. We don’t store your email in this browser or use advertising trackers. Reply to any update to leave the list.
