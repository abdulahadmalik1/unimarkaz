BEFORE THE FIRST CHAPTER

A few good
questions.

What exactly is UniMarkaz?+

UniMarkaz is a student marketplace where you can earn from what you already know. Offer tutoring, freelance skills, project collaboration, bike pooling and more — or find other students who can help you. It connects students across campuses; you set your own rates and arrange payment directly.

Is it for students from my university?+

Yes—students from any university can join the waitlist. UniMarkaz is being built for multiple campuses, so the idea is to help you connect both within and beyond your own university.

When does it launch?+

We’re building it now. There’s no fixed date to announce yet. Join the waitlist for the launch email and relevant updates as things take shape. Categories and features may evolve along the way.

What am I signing up for?+

You’re registering interest in beta testing UniMarkaz before launch. We’ll email you about testing opportunities and launch news. Joining is free, with no commitment to offer or book anything. We use your email and selected interest to keep you informed. You can ask to leave the list by replying to any update.

Can I actually make money through UniMarkaz?+

That’s exactly the idea. Whether you’re tutoring first-years, selling design work, or offering rides — UniMarkaz puts you in front of students who need what you’ve got. You set your own rates, and the money goes directly to you. We’re the connection; the income is yours.
